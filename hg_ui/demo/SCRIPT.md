# Demo video: script

`quicksilver-demo.mp4` (about 80 s). Voice: Kokoro (on-device TTS). Music: generated by `compose.py`. All notes are made-up samples.

| Scene | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| intro | Title card | Meet Quicksilver. The notebook that thinks with you, and never sends a word anywhere. |
| write | Type a messy one-line note | Real notes are messy. A meeting, an invoice, a birthday, a plumber, all in one breathless line. Just write it. |
| magic | Agent suggests a clean version, tasks and reminders; review and save | Quicksilver reads it, on your phone, and turns the chaos into a clean note. Sections. Tasks. Every date, set as a reminder. You review it, and keep your own words if you prefer. |
| find | Search 'invoice' | Looking for something? Type one word, and your notes answer instantly. |
| ask | Ask: what was that book idea about a lighthouse? | Or half remember an idea. Ask in plain words, and it brings the thought back, with the exact note it came from. |
| bills | Add and pay a bill | Bills arrive as messages. Quicksilver reads them on your phone and reminds you before they are due. Mark one paid, or add your own, and the message is deleted the moment it is settled. |
| brief | Home brief card | What needs you shows up on your home screen, and a tap opens the exact note or bill. |
| capture | Voice, photo, sketch | Speak a voice note, even in Telugu. Snap a photo. Sketch an idea. It listens, reads, and remembers. |
| themes | Cartoon, Material, Ocean, Midnight | And make it yours. Cartoon. Material. Ocean. Midnight. |
| outro | Privacy summary | Everything runs on your phone. No cloud, no account. Quicksilver. Notes that move, join, and never stain. |

Rebuild: `python3 narration.py W/narr && python3 record.py W W/narr`, then build `phone.mp4` from `frames.txt` and run `python3 compose.py W W/narr out.mp4`.
